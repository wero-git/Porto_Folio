Claims case

Analysis and modelling of a customer claim process to identify inefficiencies, bottlenecks and opportunities for improvement.

BPMN 2.0 Process Analysis Excel SQL
01 / CONTEXT

Understanding the claims process.

A customer claim passes through several processing stages, from initial registration and information collection to claim assessment, decision-making and final resolution. Each stage requires relevant case information to be available and correctly processed.

This project focuses on modelling this claim-handling workflow in BPMN, with particular attention to the activities, decision points and information flows involved in processing a claim.

The objective is to make the sequence of claim-processing activities explicit, identify where decisions and information exchanges occur, and provide a structured basis for analysing potential process improvements.

06 / OUTCOME

A clearer view of the process.

The project provides a structured representation of the claims workflow, making its activities, decisions and information flows easier to visualize and analyse.

The resulting process model provides a foundation for identifying potential optimisation opportunities to make claims cases easier and quicker to handle.

Process visibility Clearer representation of the workflow.
Structured information Clearer representation of the workflow.
Less wasted time Reduction of working time by about 35%.
Smoother work Reduction of the workload per employee, about 1,2 employees per task.
Improvement perspective More communication between the departments.
07 / KEY LEARNING

Connecting processes and data.

This project shows how BPMN modelling and structured claims data can be combined to make the claim-handling workflow more transparent, from initial registration and assessment to the final decision and case closure.
