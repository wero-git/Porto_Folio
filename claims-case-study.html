PROJECT OVERVIEW

From operational workflow to structured process

Claims management involves several interconnected activities, from the initial claim registration to analysis, decision-making and final resolution.

The purpose of this project was to analyse the process, identify opportunities for improvement and design a more structured digital approach to claims management.

The project combines process modelling with data structuring in order to create a clearer and more traceable workflow.
