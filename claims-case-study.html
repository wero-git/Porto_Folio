Claims process analysis.

A process-oriented analysis of customer claim handling, translating the workflow into a structured BPMN model and examining the information required to move a case from registration to resolution.

Workflow clarity

01 / Context

Understanding how a claim moves through the process.

A customer claim does not end with its registration. Information has to be collected and reviewed, a decision has to be reached, and the resulting case has to be completed and recorded. Each handover can affect the continuity of the process.

This case study focuses on the structure of that claim-handling workflow. The process is examined as a sequence of operational activities rather than as a collection of isolated tasks.

The objective is to make the relationship between claim information, processing activities and decision points explicit, creating a clearer basis for identifying unnecessary handovers, delays or duplicated activities.

02 / Challenge

02

Identify which claim information is required at each stage and how it supports the next processing step.

03

Process efficiency

Examine handovers, waiting points and repeated work that may make claim handling slower or less transparent.

04 / Process

Four stages of the claim-handling workflow.

The model follows the claim from its initial registration through assessment and decision-making to final resolution.

01 Registration

Receive the claim and record the information required to open the case.

→
02 Assessment

Review the available information and assess the case against the relevant requirements.

→
03 Decision

Determine the appropriate outcome or the next action required to continue processing the claim.

→
04 Resolution

Complete the case, record the outcome and bring the claim-handling cycle to an end.

06 / Outcome

The result is a structured view of the claim-handling process that connects operational activities with the information and decisions required to move a case forward.

01

02

Structure

Claim information can be organised around the stages through which a case actually moves.

03

Improvement basis

07 / Key Learning

Making the relationship between process, information and decisions visible.

The case shows how BPMN can turn a claim-handling workflow into a process structure that can be discussed, analysed and improved. Combining the process model with structured claim data adds the information perspective needed to understand how individual cases move through the workflow.
