01 / CONTEXT

Understanding the claims process

A customer claim flows through multiple stages – from initial registration and data capture, through analysis and decision-making, to final resolution. Each stage relies on accurate information being collected and transferred.

This project focuses on making that workflow explicit by mapping every activity, responsibility, and decision point. The goal is to expose the full sequence so we can identify bottlenecks and opportunities to streamline the process.

04 / PROCESS

The claim-handling flow

The workflow can be broken into four main stages:

01 Registration Claim received and basic details recorded
02 Assessment Gather documents and assess claim validity
03 Decision Determine approval or necessary actions
04 Resolution Complete claim and notify customer

06 / OUTCOME

Making the process measurable

The process model provides a clear, structured view of claim handling, making all steps, decisions and data explicit. This reveals where time is spent and how cases flow.

The analysis highlights opportunities to reduce delays (e.g. parallel tasks, better handoff) and to improve coordination and accountability in the workflow.
