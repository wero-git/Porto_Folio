PROCESS OPTIMIZATION PROJECT 01

Claims

Analysis and modelling of a customer claim-handling process, from initial registration and assessment to decision-making and case resolution.

BPMN 2.0 Process Analysis Excel SQL
01 / CONTEXT

Understanding the
claims process.

A customer claim passes through several processing stages, from initial registration and information collection to claim assessment, decision-making and final resolution.

Each stage depends on relevant case information being available, correctly processed and transferred to the next step of the workflow.

The project therefore focuses on making the claim-handling process explicit and analysing how activities, decisions and information exchanges are connected.

06 / OUTCOME

Making the process
measurable.

The process model provides a structured view of the claim-handling workflow and makes the main activities, decisions and information exchanges easier to analyse.

The analysis also highlights opportunities to reduce unnecessary processing time, improve coordination and make responsibilities within the workflow clearer.

07 / KEY LEARNING

A claims process becomes easier to improve once its flow, decisions and information are visible.

This project combines BPMN modelling with structured claims data to make the relationship between process activities, information and outcomes visible. The resulting model provides a concrete basis for analysing processing time, coordination and improvement opportunities.
